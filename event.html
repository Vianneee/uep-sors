USC, CSC PIOs joins 4th Pagsubay: A Journalism Skills Training

By University Student Council — UEP Main Campus  ·  October 30, 2025

During the culmination of the event, PIOs bagged the third placer in Editorial Writing with its piece titled "Deferral of UEPAA: Benefit in Disguise," highlighting its stance with the postponement of this year's UEPAA Games.

The 4th Pagsubay: A Journalism Skills Training gathered Public Information Officers (PIOs) from the University Student Council (USC) and College Student Councils (CSC) across the UEP Main Campus. The training was designed to sharpen the communication and journalism skills of student leaders tasked with disseminating information to the broader student body.

Participants engaged in workshops covering news writing, feature writing, editorial writing, and social media communication. Resource speakers from the field of journalism and campus publications shared their expertise and practical tips for effective student reporting.

The event also served as a platform for PIOs to network with their counterparts from different colleges, fostering collaboration and a shared commitment to transparent and accurate student communications.

The success of the 4th Pagsubay reflects the USC and CSC's ongoing dedication to building the capacity of student leaders and ensuring that UEP students remain well-informed about campus affairs and events. ¶
